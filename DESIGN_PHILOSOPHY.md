# Design Philosophy

Generous UI follows **generous clarity**: every element earns its place, and what remains is given full, confident presence on the canvas.

The interface should feel usable before it feels impressive. A child or grandparent should be able to try it without instruction. Personality lives in motion, timing, contrast, and carefully executed functional elements, not in decorative effects.

## Principles

- One thing in focus: every screen has exactly one primary verb.
- Cut first, then enlarge: remove weak elements before styling the survivors.
- Design for instinct: use conventional anchors and controls.
- Self-explanatory affordances: buttons look pressable, links look clickable, disabled things look disabled.
- Anticipatory layout: the next useful action should already be where the user expects it.
- Familiar mental models: channels, shelves, cards, files, folders, and remotes beat novel interaction paradigms.
- High contrast and bold hierarchy: size and weight do more work than subtle grey-on-grey styling.
- Warmth through motion, not ornament: animate only to communicate state.
- One signature device: choose one recurring idea per product, or none.
- Spatial stability: reserve dimensions before async content arrives.
- Robust interactions: fast clicks, slow networks, and edge cases must not break trust.
- Honest interfaces: no dark patterns, fake urgency, fake progress, or fake capability.
- Long-lasting defaults: avoid trends that date the product.

## Defaults

- System fonts.
- Neutral palette with one accent.
- Sharp or modest corners.
- Surgical shadows.
- No emoji in UI chrome.
- No glassmorphism, gradient text, glow effects, or AI shimmer.
- Minimum 44x44px touch targets.
- Specific, human errors.
- Top-left for back/home, top-right for close/account, bottom or thumb-reach for primary actions.
- Buttons use verbs.
- Toasts overlay content.
- Popovers and menus anchor to their trigger.

## Refusals

Do not default to gradient backgrounds, rainbow text, frosted cards, glow effects, excessive rounding, floating navigation islands, decorative motion, card-everything layouts, or async layout shift.
